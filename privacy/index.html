Privacy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

The short version: yuzudo collects no personal data. There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. Everything you do in the app stays on your phone, and no data is sent to us.

The app

yuzudo works the same way on the iPhone and on Android. Where the two differ, this page says so.

Your data stays on your device

Your timers, fruits, focus minutes and history are stored only on your phone. yuzudo sends no data to us.

Notifications, widgets and the Lock Screen

When a timer is up, yuzudo tells you with a notification that your phone creates itself. It doesn’t come through the internet, and no server is involved.

On the iPhone, a running timer also shows as a Live Activity on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island. yuzudo’s widgets for the Home Screen and the Lock Screen, and its control for the Lock Screen, Control Center or the Action button, show your timers. All of them get your timers from files in yuzudo’s App Group: a storage area on your iPhone that only yuzudo and its widgets share. These files stay on your iPhone, apart from your own backup.

iPhone backup and transfer

If you back up your iPhone to iCloud or to a computer, the backup includes yuzudo’s data: your timers, fruits, minutes, history and settings, and the files for the widgets. When you move to a new iPhone with Apple’s transfer from phone to phone (Quick Start), this data comes along too. A backup in iCloud is handled by Apple under Apple’s privacy policy, and a backup on your computer stays there. We have no access to either.

Android backup

If backup is turned on for your phone, Android may include yuzudo’s data in your device backup, which is stored in your Google account. That backup is handled by Google under Google’s privacy policy. We have no access to it.

Purchases

yuzudo+ is a one-time purchase. On the iPhone, you buy it in the App Store, through Apple’s StoreKit: Apple shows its own purchase sheet and processes the payment. On Android, you buy it through Google Play Billing, and Google processes the payment. Either way, the app only checks on your phone whether you own yuzudo+. We receive no payment details, and no server of ours is involved.

Save / Load progress

In Settings you can save your progress to a file and load it again, for example on a new phone. This is optional. The file is encrypted. To lock it to you, it contains a salted SHA-256 hash: on Android of the Google account name you pick, on the iPhone of an email address of yours that you type in. The app turns the name or address into this hash inside the encrypted file and never stores or sends the name or address itself. yuzudo doesn’t sign in to any account. The file stays wherever you keep it (on the iPhone, you pick the place in the share sheet, for example Files, iCloud Drive, Google Drive, AirDrop or Mail) and is not sent to us.

Children

yuzudo is made for ages 9 and up. The app collects no personal data from anyone, including children.

This website

yuzudo.app sets no cookies, uses no analytics and loads nothing from third parties. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc. GitHub processes visitors’ IP addresses in its server logs to deliver the site and for security, under GitHub’s own privacy statement. The domain’s DNS is served by Cloudflare, which answers DNS lookups only and does not proxy this website.

Email

If you write to hi@yuzudo.app, we use your email address and your message only to answer you.

Your rights

Under the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP) and the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), you can ask which personal data we hold about you and have it corrected or deleted. We hold no app data about you. For anything else, such as emails you sent us, write to hi@yuzudo.app. You can also lodge a complaint with a data protection authority.

Contact

Questions about privacy: hi@yuzudo.app

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
