Privacy

Last updated: 18 October 2026

The short version: yuzudo collects no personal data. There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. Everything you do in the app stays on your phone, and no data is sent to us.

The app

Your data stays on your device

Your timers, fruits, focus minutes and history are stored only on your phone. yuzudo sends no data to us.

Android backup

If backup is turned on for your phone, Android may include yuzudo’s data in your device backup, which is stored in your Google account. That backup is handled by Google under Google’s privacy policy. We have no access to it.

Purchases

yuzudo+ is a one-time purchase made through Google Play Billing. Google processes the payment. We receive no payment details.

Save / Load progress

In Settings you can save your progress to a file and load it again, for example on a new phone. This is optional. The file is encrypted. To lock it to you, it contains a salted hash of the Google account name you pick, never the name itself. The file stays wherever you keep it and is not sent to us.

Children

yuzudo is made for ages 9 and up. The app collects no personal data from anyone, including children.

This website

yuzudo.app sets no cookies, uses no analytics and loads nothing from third parties. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc. GitHub processes visitors’ IP addresses in its server logs to deliver the site and for security, under GitHub’s own privacy statement. The domain’s DNS is served by Cloudflare, which answers DNS lookups only and does not proxy this website.

Email

If you write to hi@yuzudo.app, we use your email address and your message only to answer you.

Your rights

Under the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP) and the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), you can ask which personal data we hold about you and have it corrected or deleted. We hold no app data about you. For anything else, such as emails you sent us, write to hi@yuzudo.app. You can also lodge a complaint with a data protection authority.

Contact

Questions about privacy: hi@yuzudo.app

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
