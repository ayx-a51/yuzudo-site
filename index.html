the fruity focus timer

Every timer is a fruit that ripens while you focus. Finished timers fill your crates, and new fruits grow as you go. For homework, practice or work, for anyone aged 9 and up.

Coming soon to Google Play

Android first. An iOS version is planned for later.

Every timer is a fruit

Pick a fruit, set the minutes and start. While the timer runs, the fruit ripens: slices wedge by wedge, whole fruits from the bottom up. When the time is up, it’s ripe. Pause whenever you need a break.

  1. 25:00start
  2. 15:0010 minutes in
  3. 0:00ripe!

Finished timers fill crates

Every fruit you ripen goes into today’s crate. The week’s crates line up on a market stall, one for each day. And the quarter rolls by as a convoy: one vehicle per week, from a wheelbarrow to a big truck, depending on how much you focused.

120 fruits to collect

You start with a few fruits on your shelf. Your focus time grows new ones: the little sprout on the home screen shows how many minutes are left until the next. Berries, citrus, tropical fruits, vegetables, nuts and even a few flowers, 120 in all.

No fruit is for sale. Every one grows from your own focus time.
