Support

Answers to the most common questions about yuzudo. Can’t find yours? Write to hi@yuzudo.app.

How do the timers work?

Each timer on your shelf is a fruit with its own number of minutes. Start one, and the fruit ripens while the timer runs. You can pause or stop it at any time. To change a timer’s minutes, name or fruit, tap the pencil on its label. Use the empty slot with the plus to add a timer, and long-press the home screen to rearrange your timers.

How do I get new fruits?

They grow from your focus time. The sprout pot next to today’s crate shows how many minutes are left until your next fruit. Only timers that run to the end count. When a new fruit is ready, you can pick it for your timers straight away. Fruits with a lock are yuzudo+ fruits.

What does yuzudo+ include?

yuzudo+ gives you up to 9 timers on three shelves instead of 4, all 120 fruits, and your whole history, including the quarter view in Statistics. Without yuzudo+, the quarter view shows a locked preview. yuzudo+ is a one-time purchase, not a subscription. There are no ads in yuzudo, with or without yuzudo+.

I have a new phone. How do I get yuzudo+ back?

Your purchase is linked to the Google account you used on Google Play. Install yuzudo on the new phone with the same Google account, and yuzudo+ is available again through Google Play.

How do I move my fruits and history to a new phone?

There are two ways. Android’s own backup can bring yuzudo’s data along when you set up the new phone with your Google account. Or use Save / Load progress in Settings: save a progress file on the old phone, move it to the new one and load it there. The file is encrypted and locked to the Google account you pick, so it only loads with that account.

Do I need an account?

No. yuzudo has no sign-up and no login, and nothing is sent to us. Your data stays on your phone.

How do I delete my data?

Uninstall yuzudo, and its data is removed from your phone. To start over without uninstalling, open Settings and use Reset progress in the danger zone at the very bottom. A copy in your Android backup is managed in your Google account, and progress files you saved yourself stay wherever you put them until you delete them.

Something isn’t working. Who can I ask?

Write to hi@yuzudo.app. If something isn’t working, please tell us your phone model, your Android version and what happened.
